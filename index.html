Sentiment Summary

No Pave-specific articles, reviews, or discussions were published or updated in the last 7 days, so this week's score is carried forward unchanged from the standing baseline built from coverage since Pave's April 28, 2026 launch. That baseline is cautiously positive: outlets and early users consistently praise Pave for compressing "idea to working app" timelines and for shipping enterprise governance (SSO, permissions, audit trails, version rollback) by default rather than bolted on (GlobeNewswire, Silicon Snark). The New Stack frames Pave as a direct answer to vibe-coding's "80% problem," where AI-generated prototypes stall before production (The New Stack). Countering that, independent analysis argues Pave's guardrails cover traditional access/audit risk but not agentic-AI-specific risks like goal hijacking, and that output quality is capped by how well a user can articulate the problem up front (VeilSun). No dedicated G2, TrustRadius, or Capterra review volume for Pave specifically was found yet (the product is ~10 weeks old); the G2 Pave reviews listing exists at g2.com/products/quickbase-pave/reviews but returned no accessible review content this week.
